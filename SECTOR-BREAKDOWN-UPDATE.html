Simplified sector journey breakdown

The visible breakdown prioritizes passenger sector block time, modelled ground turnaround, selected intermediate stops and unlocated technical/fuel stops. Each itinerary shows one consolidated indicative charter price range, with no line-item financial accounting or per-sector euro charges.

The underlying estimator pricing formula and its auditable calculation dataset were not changed. Ground waiting is in passenger journey time, not billable flight hours. Aircraft positioning is a cost allowance only; it is not part of the passenger journey.

This is planning-only. Fuel-stop sites, airport availability, routing, weather, fees and operator pricing require separate confirmation.
