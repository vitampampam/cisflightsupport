Stopover pricing and touch-carousel change

Flight estimator

  • Separates flight block time from non-billable ground turnaround. The reference turnaround assumption is 60 min per technical stop, counted only toward total journey duration. Ground turnaround has a zero flight-hour charge.
  • Additional fuel stops require additional flight operations: a configurable 30-min billable block-time allowance per unlocated fuel stop (extra takeoff, taxi, approach and climb/descent), not one hour of parked time.
  • Each manually entered intermediate airport starts a new flight leg; each leg carries its own 30-minute terminal/taxi allowance.
  • The existing configurable €2,500 fuel/technical-stop amount remains an illustrative combined service fee (landing, handling/refuelling service), not a verified airport fee or the price of the uplifted fuel. Only the base origin/destination handling allowance is charged separately.
  • Return itineraries count the actual outbound and inbound legs, stop ground times, fees and overnight assumptions in both directions.
  • The detailed breakdown shows billable block time, zero-priced turnaround, technical-stop allowances and other pricing assumptions, with reconciliation against the total price.
  • Estimations are not certified routes or quotes; fuel, landing permissions, ground services, operator terms and minimums require confirmation.

Tablet and phone carousels

  • The previous/next arrow controls are hidden on iPhone and iPad (touch pointers), including the services and gallery carousels.
  • Touch swiping, snap positioning and pagination dots remain. Desktop arrow navigation is retained.
