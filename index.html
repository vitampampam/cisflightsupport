About CIS Flight Support

An aviation services company, not a single-service agency

For more than twenty years CIS Flight Support has worked for private owners, corporate flight departments and regional operators across Europe — handling and dispatching their flights, advising on maintenance and regulations, arranging charters, and buying and selling the aircraft themselves. We work in small numbers and stay on a file from the first conversation to the last signature.

What sets the firm apart is the technical side. Records reviews, borescope findings and maintenance forecasts are produced in house by people with maintenance and CAMO backgrounds, rather than bought in at the last minute. The same expertise now runs the CAW Academy training platform.

  • Ground handling supervision
  • Passenger and VIP services
  • Flight planning, slots and permits
  • Charter sourcing and operator vetting
  • Maintenance scoping and shop-visit oversight
  • Market valuation and needs analysis
  • Aircraft search, listing and negotiation
  • Pre-buy inspection supervision
  • Import, export and registration
  • Regulatory compliance and audit preparation
