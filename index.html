About CIS Flight Support

An aviation services company, not a single-service agency

For more than twenty years CIS Flight Support has worked for private owners, corporate flight departments and regional operators across Europe — handling and dispatching their flights, advising on maintenance and regulations, arranging charters, and buying and selling the aircraft themselves. We work in small numbers and stay on a file from the first conversation to the last signature.

What sets the firm apart is the technical side. Records reviews, borescope findings and maintenance forecasts are produced in house by people with maintenance and CAMO backgrounds, rather than bought in at the last minute. The same expertise now runs the CAW Academy training platform.

Pre-purchase inspection

How a pre-buy runs

Five to ten working days from start to report, depending on type, records condition and the maintenance organisation's slot.

Records first

Logbooks, dirty fingerprint, AD and SB status, life-limited parts, damage and repair history. Most deals that fail, fail here — before anyone travels.

On the aircraft

Physical survey with the maintenance organisation: engines borescoped, structure and corrosion checked, systems run, cabin and avionics photographed.

Ground and flight checks

Functional checks and, where the deal calls for it, an acceptance flight with our engineer on board recording what the aircraft actually does.

Report and numbers

Findings split into airworthiness items, negotiation items and things to live with, each costed so you can put the figures in front of the seller.
