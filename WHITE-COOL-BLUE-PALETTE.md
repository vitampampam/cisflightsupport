# CIS Flight Support — white and cool soft blue

The old steel-blue had a teal/green undertone. This redesign uses genuinely cool blue hues (roughly 220°) on white. No aqua, teal, champagne, or greenish-blue brand accents.

- Main white background: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary medium blue CTA: `#4C6FB2`
- CTA hover: `#395B98`
- Headings: `#263956`
- Soft-blue cards: `#EEF3FF`
- Lightest blue surface: `#F7F9FF`
- Borders: `#DCE5F7`
- Accent: `#9FB9E8`

The stylesheet colors, browser theme color, and HTML stylesheet cache version are updated. The estimator overlay also receives a small CSS stacking-context safeguard. Fonts, dimensions, responsive layouts, estimator scripts, and all 35 aircraft models are unchanged. CAW Academy remains purple.
