How a pre-buy runs

Five to ten working days from start to report, depending on type, records condition and the maintenance organisation's slot.

Records first

Logbooks, dirty fingerprint, AD and SB status, life-limited parts, damage and repair history. Most deals that fail, fail here — before anyone travels.

On the aircraft

Physical survey with the maintenance organisation: engines borescoped, structure and corrosion checked, systems run, cabin and avionics photographed.

Ground and flight checks

Functional checks and, where the deal calls for it, an acceptance flight with our engineer on board recording what the aircraft actually does.

Report and numbers

Findings split into airworthiness items, negotiation items and things to live with, each costed so you can put the figures in front of the seller.

Selling your aircraft

A valuation, a marketing plan and a realistic timeline — before you commit to anything.
